Delete my account

You can delete your STORYLAND account and the data in it at any time.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open STORYLAND and sign in.
  2. Tap the menu button (☰) at the top right.
  3. Choose Delete my account and enter your password to confirm.

Option 2: by email

Email storylandconnect@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my STORYLAND account". We will confirm and delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted:
  • Your parent account and sign-in details
  • All child profiles
  • Listening history
  • Stories you created
  • Cloned voices and voice recordings (also removed from our voice partner)
  • Auto-renew is stopped, so you are not charged again
What is kept: payment records (amount, date, reference) only for as long as tax and accounting law requires. They are no longer linked to your name or email.

Deletion cannot be undone. If you only want to remove a voice or a child profile, you can do that in the app without deleting the account.
