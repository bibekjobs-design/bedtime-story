Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

STORYLAND ("we", "us") is a story app for children, used by their parents or guardians. This page explains what we collect, why, who helps us, and the choices you have. Questions: storylandconnect@gmail.com.

In short: parents create the account. We keep only what the app needs to work. We never sell data and we show no ads. Stories narrated in a cloned voice are deleted automatically after 10 days, and you can delete your whole account any time.

Who the app is for

STORYLAND is designed for children, but accounts are created and managed only by a parent or guardian. Children do not create accounts, and we do not ask a child for their email, phone number or location. Parent-only areas (profiles, voice cloning, plans) are protected by a parental gate.

What we collect

  • Parent account: name, email address, mobile number, and an encrypted (hashed) password.
  • Child profiles: a first name or nickname and an age group, used to choose suitable stories and to personalise narration. Please use a nickname if you prefer.
  • Voice recordings: only if a parent chooses to clone their voice. See "Voice cloning" below.
  • Stories and listening history: stories created for your account, which stories were played and when, and the language and narrator chosen.
  • Payments: plan, amount, date and payment reference. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by Razorpay and are never stored by us.
  • Technical data: basic request logs (such as time and error messages) needed to keep the service running.
  • Optional marketing emails: only if you tick the box when signing up. You can opt out any time.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sign-in, stories, narration, history, plans.
  • To create AI stories and audio you ask for.
  • To take payment, prevent misuse and give support.
  • To send account emails (verification, password reset) and, only if you agreed, story recommendations.

Voice cloning

  • A parent may record a short voice sample so stories can be read in that voice. This is optional and needs the parent's explicit consent in the app before anything is sent.
  • The recording is stored securely and sent to our voice partner, ElevenLabs, to create the voice. It is used only to narrate stories for your family.
  • Your cloned voice stays until you delete it in the app (or delete your account), and the recording is deleted with it.
  • Automatic deletion of stories: stories narrated in your cloned voice are deleted 10 days after they are created, from our storage and your History. You can save them to your device before then.
  • Do not record anyone else's voice without their agreement.

Who we share data with

Only the services that help us run STORYLAND, each for its own purpose:

  • Supabase: database and file storage.
  • Google (Cloud Text-to-Speech and Gemini): turning story text into speech and writing or translating stories.
  • ElevenLabs: parent voice cloning and cloned-voice narration.
  • Razorpay: payments.
  • Render: hosting the app's servers.
  • Email delivery service: sending account emails.

We do not sell personal data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles of children. We may disclose data if required by law.

How long we keep it

  • Account, child profiles and history: until you delete the account.
  • Cloned voice and its recording: until you delete them or your account.
  • Stories narrated in your cloned voice: 10 days after they are created.
  • Payment records: kept as long as tax and accounting law requires, then removed.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data: in the app (menu, "Delete my account") or at this page.
  • Access or correct your data, or stop marketing emails: email us.
  • Delete a child profile or a voice: in the app's profiles area.

Security

Passwords are hashed, connections use HTTPS, and stored files are access-controlled. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you if something affecting you goes wrong.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
